Path to Menzoberranzan

Role: Integration Programmer

Path to Menzoberranzan is a custom campaign mod for Baldur’s Gate 3. Team is creating an all-new, three-act story featuring iconic Forgotten Realms locations, faithfully recreated in the BG3 engine, a new protagonist and party of companions, full voice acting and more. This project is a community driven mod and everybody collaborates for free on their own schedule. It will be free to play. PTM team is not associated or partnered with Larian and other entities who authored or own Baldur's Gate 3 and D&D.
